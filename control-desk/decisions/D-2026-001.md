# Decision Record: Hosted SaaS and Self-Hosted Deployment

## Decision

STS Control Desk should be designed for both:

1. A hosted, multi-tenant SaaS deployment for convenience and collaboration.
2. A self-hosted or local deployment for sensitive, regulated, offline, or isolated projects.

## Authority boundary

Project repositories remain authoritative. The hosted or self-hosted Control Desk stores derived projections, control records, evidence references, and audit history according to an explicitly approved synchronization boundary.

## Not authorized by this decision

- Production hosting or external publication.
- Automatic repository synchronization.
- Automatic approvals, execution, deployment, or scope changes.
- Ingesting complete repository contents by default.

## Rationale

The dual-deployment model preserves the local-first control boundary while allowing organizations that accept hosted infrastructure to collaborate through a shared dashboard.
