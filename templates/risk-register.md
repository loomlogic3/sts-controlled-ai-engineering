Use this register to identify, evaluate, control, monitor, and close risks throughout a project.

A risk is not resolved merely because it has been documented. Every material risk requires an owner, control, evidence, and review decision.

## Risk Classification

### Likelihood

- Rare — Not expected under normal conditions
- Unlikely — Possible but not anticipated
- Possible — Could occur
- Likely — Expected in some conditions
- Almost certain — Expected without additional control

### Impact

- Minor — Limited inconvenience or easily reversible effect
- Moderate — Meaningful disruption requiring correction
- Major — Serious operational, data, financial, or security effect
- Severe — Broad damage, loss, or prolonged outage
- Critical — Irreversible, safety-sensitive, or system-wide consequence

### Control priority

- Low — Monitor through normal engineering checks
- Medium — Add defined controls and verification
- High — Require explicit approval, recovery planning, and strong evidence
- Critical — Block execution until separately approved and preflight verified
